Privacy Policy for CamHunt: Hidden Camera Detector

Developer: DB App Studio
Application: CamHunt: Hidden Camera Detector (com.camhunt.hiddencameradetector)
Contact: camhuntapp.support@gmail.com
Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. What this policy covers

CamHunt is an assistant for reviewing possible hidden-camera clues. This policy explains the information used by its checks, report sharing and support features, and by the Google advertising, consent and billing services built into the app.

CamHunt has no user account and no developer-operated scan server. Camera frames are processed in memory and are not saved as photos or recordings or uploaded for analysis. CamHunt does not provide a saved scan-report history, and does not send reports, PDFs or screenshots to DB App Studio. Sharing a report uses temporary files, as described in section 4. Google services process information as described in section 6, and we receive information that you choose to email to support (section 5).

2. Camera and sensor checks

Lens Reflection, Infrared (IR) and AI Object Scan process live camera frames on your phone. The AI Object Scan model is included in the app. Frames are processed in memory, are not saved as photographs or recordings, and are not uploaded for analysis.

The Magnetometer check uses the phone's magnetic sensor. Lens Reflection can also use accelerometer and gyroscope readings to judge movement and steadiness. These readings are processed on your phone and are not saved.

The IR sensitivity check runs only when you start it, and its result is kept only while that screen is open. It is not stored as a permanent record of your phone's camera.

3. Network and Bluetooth checks

Network Scanner examines devices through the network your phone is connected to, using connection attempts and service responses. It uses connection information such as local IP addresses, the network's address range and the connected Wi-Fi name. Probes are sent only through the network CamHunt has selected and confirmed; if it cannot confirm which network the phone is connected to, the Network check does not run. The scanner is intended for networks you own or are authorised to inspect. Some discovery requests use unencrypted local-device protocols; they do not contain camera frames, reports, support messages or payment details. The app does not follow HTTP redirects from probed devices. Network results are processed on your phone and are not automatically uploaded to us.

Bluetooth Scanner listens for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements. It can use device names and addresses, advertised services and other advertisement information, and signal strength to produce clues. It does not connect to a camera feed, record another device, or use these observations to work out your physical location. These observations are processed on your phone and are not saved. If you choose to share a report, its summary may include device-identifying information relevant to the result.

4. Reports: no saved history, temporary sharing files

CamHunt does not provide a saved report history. Scan results are held in memory for the current scan and report. After Scan All, Network Scanner or Bluetooth Scanner, a report is shown on screen; it may include a timestamp, scan duration, scores, evidence descriptions, coverage and the reasons a check did not run, network names, local device addresses and device clues. The report is discarded when you leave that screen.

You can share a report as a screenshot or, with Pro, as a PDF. When you tap Share, CamHunt creates a separate temporary file in its private cache so the app you choose can read it. Files older than one hour are removed the next time the app starts, a report closes or another share happens, so cleanup can happen later than one hour if the app is not used. Closing a report does not immediately revoke a file another app may still be reading. These files are not a report history, are not written to your gallery by CamHunt, are excluded from Android backup and are not uploaded by CamHunt. Clearing CamHunt's cache also removes them. The copy you share is handled by the app or service you choose.

Earlier versions of CamHunt could save reports and PDFs on your device. Those files are deleted automatically the first time you open this version. Screenshots that an earlier version saved to your gallery are your files and are not removed by CamHunt; you can delete them with your gallery app.

The app also stores ordinary settings on your phone: your theme, onboarding state, alert preferences, and cached Pro status with the time Google Play last confirmed it. Some ordinary settings may be included in Android backup under your device settings; subscription-related data is excluded.

5. Support and feedback

The Support screen asks for an email address and a message. You may select up to four images using Android's photo picker. The app reads only the images you select, for previews and attachments; it does not browse your photo library. A support draft includes the app version, device manufacturer and model, and Android version to help diagnose problems. Feedback shortcuts can also prepare a draft with these app and device details. You review the draft in your email app and choose whether to send it.

If you send a message, we receive its contents, your email details, any attachments you chose and the included diagnostic information. Our support inbox is hosted by Gmail; your own email app and provider also process the message. Please do not send payment details or passwords.

6. Advertising and consent

The free version of CamHunt shows Google AdMob advertising. Active Pro access removes advertising. Google's advertising services may process your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, advertising and device identifiers, app and device information, interactions with the app or ads, and advertising diagnostics. Google uses this information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. We do not receive individual scan results from these services.

CamHunt uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for and update your advertising privacy choices where this is required. Declining personalised advertising may still allow limited or non-personalised advertising and the technical processing it needs. Where Google requires it and ads are enabled, Settings → Privacy choices lets you change your choices at any time; this entry does not appear where no such form is required, or while Pro removes ads. Android also lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.

7. Intended audience and advertising

CamHunt is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The app does not ask for your age range and does not verify individual users’ ages. Google Play target-audience settings are configured separately.

For its declared adult audience, CamHunt does not enable child-directed or under-age-of-consent ad treatment, and limits ad content to teen-appropriate ratings. This does not verify any individual user's age. The Google consent service is still used where required, as described in section 6. Older versions may have stored an age-range choice on your phone; this version does not read or use that choice.

8. Purchases

Google Play handles subscription checkout and payment. CamHunt receives product identifiers, purchase state, acknowledgement state and purchase tokens to manage Pro access. We do not receive your card details, payment method or billing address. CamHunt caches confirmation of your subscription for temporary offline access and normally requires a new confirmation after 14 days since the last confirmation. A change to the device clock may require earlier verification. Before acknowledging a completed purchase, CamHunt stores its token in app-private storage excluded from Android backup and device transfer. It attempts acknowledgement and schedules retries, subject to network availability and Android's background restrictions; if you force-stop the app, retries resume only after you open it again. It removes queued tokens when acknowledgement succeeds or a current ownership check shows that acknowledgement is no longer needed. Pending payments are not acknowledged until they complete. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play. Uninstalling CamHunt does not cancel it.

9. Permissions

  • Camera — for the three camera checks.
  • Location — to read Wi-Fi connection information and, on Android 11 and earlier, to discover Bluetooth devices. CamHunt explains this before asking, does not request GPS coordinates, and does not use these checks to find your position. It can check whether system Location is on, because Android can require it for these features. Without it, the affected checks do not run and are reported as not run.
  • Nearby devices — for Bluetooth scanning and device names on Android 12 and later. Bluetooth scanning is declared as not used for location.
  • Wi-Fi and network state, and Internet — for connection checks, local device probing and Google services.
  • Vibration — for feedback.
  • Advertising ID — added by the Google ads library.

CamHunt does not request microphone, contacts, SMS, call-log or background-location access.

10. Retention, deletion and security

CamHunt has no saved report history. Temporary sharing files are cleaned up as described in section 4; clearing the app's cache also removes them. Copies you shared are held by the apps or services that received them; delete them there. Clearing app data removes locally cached subscription status but does not erase Google Play's purchase records or cancel billing. We keep support messages only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and related obligations. Email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold; we aim to respond within 30 days, subject to any verification needed to identify your message.

Android protects app-private storage. Google's services use their own security controls. Some local network discovery uses unencrypted protocols, as described in section 3. Your email provider controls email transport. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Google decides how long it keeps information processed under its services.
